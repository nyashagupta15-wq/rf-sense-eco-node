    How the data was made. A script simulated 16 nodes (8 in Madhya Pradesh, 8 in Chhattisgarh) for 14 days (7 to 20 Oct 2026, IST). Each node wakes every 15 minutes and takes a reading only if its supercapacitor holds 10 mJ. Harvested power is RF input times conversion efficiency, taken as 16% at -15 dBm and rising 2.5 points per dB, in line with reported rectifier efficiencies of 16 to 41% between -15 and -5 dBm.

    What is and is not built in. Bird activity follows a dawn and dusk pattern with a site baseline and a weather factor. It is generated independently of RF level, so any trend in the scatter chart is noise. A real deployment may show any pattern, and that is what the pilot is designed to find out.

    Status thresholds. Reporting is 80 or more readings per day, reduced rate is 36 to 79, and too little RF is under 36. A site survey before installation would screen out the last group.
